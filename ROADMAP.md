What is in progress, what is next, and what was decided. A finished item is deleted (git history has it); features are described in [docs/](docs/).

## Decisions
- **One repository** for the game, its Archipelago client and its world. The world lives in `apworld/ctr`, imported with its history from [dowlle/ctr-archipelago-apworld](https://github.com/dowlle/ctr-archipelago-apworld) (`worlds/ctr`), so client and world are versioned together.
- **Base: dowlle/ctr-native-ap** `main` (v0.2.4) plus its unreleased 0.3.0 line (`dev/custom-pad-0.3.0`, the signed updater, Play Solo, the xdelta importer). Seeds made with `ctr.apworld` 0.2.4 still work.
- **Custom racers are new slots, not replacements.** Each has its own icon, name, model and textures, and borrows a retail character ID (its template) only for systems keyed by that ID (voice, podium, records, Archipelago). Real IDs above 15 would touch about a hundred sites, save formats included (a memory card ghost packs the ID into 4 bits). Portraits go to a second VRAM bank, so the template keeps its own and can race in the same field (High Octane overwrites the template's textures, its #36).
- **Racers ship from [Decryptu/ctr-decrypted-racers](https://github.com/Decryptu/ctr-decrypted-racers)**, an index the Mods screen downloads from, like Saphi for tracks. Each racer is credited to its author. A package holds only the mod's own model and portrait, nothing from the player's disc; a racer that reuses a look from another release credits it.
- **Tracks also ship from [Decryptu/ctr-decrypted-tracks](https://github.com/Decryptu/ctr-decrypted-tracks)**, listed with Saphi's on the Tracks tab, so a track need not wait for Saphi. A track both list shows once, with Saphi's row winning a tie ([CUSTOM_TRACKS.md](docs/CUSTOM_TRACKS.md#the-decrypted-track-catalogue)).
- **The Mods screen has no Karts tab**: a racer's model is driver and kart in one mesh, and wheels are shared sprites (owner decision, 2026-10-31).
- **60 FPS simulates at 60 Hz** (owner decision, 2026-11-02). A step that runs once per frame instead of per unit of time is fixed where it happens ([60FPS.md](docs/60FPS.md)). No fixed 30 Hz simulation with interpolated rendering: it would bring back 33 ms input steps and touch every rendered position.
- **High Octane is ported feature by feature, never merged.** A trial merge conflicts in 92 files, and it carries Vita, web and leaderboard code we don't want.

## In progress
- Mario Kart DS course converter ([TRACK-CONVERTER.md](docs/TRACK-CONVERTER.md)). All 32 race courses convert and finish laps on autopilot. Next steps, in this order:
  1. The owner's replays of all 32. Treated since the last one, and installed in the test game: confirm or reopen each row, then drop what is gone.
     - **Kart bumps and jump heights** (Delfino Square, DK Pass, GBA Bowser Castle 2, Peach Gardens): every fruit or crate touched in a frame gave the kart a further frame of movement, in the game's movement sweep, so retail tracks change a little too. Confirm, with the older reports of a kart launched on the road: GCN Mushroom Bridge at about 14919,3928, N64 Banshee Boardwalk at about 6932,9008.
     - **Aku Aku:** ground a quarter of a lap from a kart's progress holds that progress instead of calling him, so GCN Luigi Circuit's whole road can be driven and a cut lap does not count. Stray end-of-lap checkpoints that made N64 Frappe Snowland's cut are gone. Confirm both.
     - **Mud:** Wario Stadium, SNES Choco Island 2 and SNES Donut Plains 1 take Tiny Arena's `Mud` terrain, splashes included, and neither it nor water raises the camera on a converted course. Confirm how it looks and slows, and that a kart keeps rolling when it lets go of the gas.
     - **CPU karts** take boost pads and ramps, fly off ramps and jump pads, and follow flights along their lanes, upright: see how it looks on Airship Fortress, Rainbow Road and Waluigi Pinball. They drive through the new still obstacles. Their models disappearing on DK Pass, and riding through the ground on Airship Fortress, were lanes under the crests between two nodes, up to 52 MKDS units, now 2: confirm.
     - **Bowser Castle:** the turning room, the slabs over the lava and the log were wrongly mapped or missing, and now stand still at their MKDS places. No flights are left there. Play the whole lap.
     - **Delfino Square:** the drawbridge stands 12 degrees open, each half a ramp of dash panels. Judge the jump and the angle (`OPEN_BRIDGE_ANGLE`). Only the deck is drawn: its chains and posts led to towers the course does not have.
     - **Water:** the sky under the horizon takes the sea's colour and what lay under the sea is gone, and the sea takes small blocks near the road, as large ones vanished beside the camera. Delfino Square's sea, the foam there and on Cheep Cheep Beach, and N64 Banshee Boardwalk's are one opaque texture. GCN Yoshi Circuit's water is drawn from below too: confirm, and how Cheep Cheep Beach's sea looks now it hides the sea bed.
     - **Flights:** Rainbow Road's loops run out along the road, Waluigi Pinball's cannon flies inside its tube and ends over its floor, the kart no longer flickers, and rank and WRONG WAY are right after a landing: confirm, and how a flight feels overall (speed, height, camera). The owner's reports of it on Rainbow Road while riding normally are taken to be this: confirm.
     - **N64 Choco Mountain's white holes:** pixel gaps at joins, 31 in 165 frames of a lap, now show the ground's colour, and 6 are left, the largest the bright end of a tunnel. The count: white clusters fully ringed by geometry. Confirm.
     - **Small fixes to confirm:** Waluigi Pinball's blue wall (the backdrop cutting through the course), its table's gold arrow strips over the blue board (checked against a DS screenshot) and the same blending for Cheep Cheep Beach's tree shadows, Rainbow Road's blotchy ground and the two-tone roads the colour reduction left on every course, GBA Sky Garden's sunken item box. Its trees stand on clouds of the backdrop: confirm they sit on them.
     - **Translucent surfaces:** Yoshi Falls' waterfalls and most of Rainbow Road blend half and half and are brightened to MKDS's share of them, and Rainbow Road's rising ring has low edges: judge both. The autopilot still flies off the ring's lower side about once a lap, near LEV (-1300, 1150, 12500), where the road is banked 50 degrees and carries on unbroken: see in play whether a player does too.
     - **Moving objects:** rebuilt from the game's own tables and code ([TRACK-CONVERTER.md](docs/TRACK-CONVERTER.md#objects)): every object has its real model, hit shape, speed and timing. Replay every course. On Tick-Tock Clock, see the end's two cogs on the green axle and whether the four floor cogs carry a kart as they should.
     - **Moving objects left as they are:** moving item boxes stand still where their path nears the route (a crate's hitbox is in the static BSP); Bowser Castle's slabs and Delfino Square's drawbridge do not travel, as the engine's track collision is static. Read in the game's code and not built yet: Chain Chomp chains and stakes, DK Pass snowballs growing as they roll, tyres turning, the sun and blimp staying hidden until lap 2 (that this is the lap number is inferred), fire plants' shots along each of their paths (s0 to s6), a Thwomp's wobble before it drops and an Iron Ball's speed by path point. Still to read: the sun's fire interval, Pakkun's cycle, the coffin, the bats' second state.
     - **Pictures:** an installed converted course shows its picture on the Mods screen, drawn from the ROM at the start line (`picture_view`): judge the view.
     - **Frame rate:** all 32 fit 16.7 ms at the owner's settings, moving objects included: median 2.4 to 7.9 ms, worst 1% at most 14.5 ms, no driver stalls, and Figure-8 Circuit has the most frames over it, 0.3%. One frame of about 49 ms shows on each of N64 Moo Moo Farm and GCN Baby Park: see whether they hitch in play.
     - **Left from earlier replays, no report either way:** thin lines on GCN Mushroom Bridge's last tunnel; item boxes reached from the air; how strong boost ramps are; SNES Donut Plains 1's wooden bridge texture when riding on it; the flicker on GBA Luigi Circuit, GCN Baby Park, Luigi's Mansion, Mario Circuit, Shroom Ridge, Yoshi Falls; the ghost shadows on GBA Peach Circuit; the minimap fill's strength; the camera on Cheep Cheep Beach's boost jump. Custom tracks are one-player only: if they ever get split screen, its far emitter (`DrawLevelOvr1P_EmitSplitGroundListAQuadBlock`) must draw by face too.
  2. Publish the finished tracks in the catalogue (`tools/tracks/build_index.py`) and submit them to Saphi, then set `saphi_track_id` on any upload whose bytes differ. A catalogue row not yet installed has no picture until the listing carries one (owner reminder, 2026-11-05).

## Maybe
- 200cc beyond Arcade and VS ([SPEED-CLASS.md](docs/SPEED-CLASS.md)): Time Trial with records and ghosts kept per class (a save format change), and whether Archipelago can require or place it.
- Title screen: a drawn logo in place of the DECRYPTED text, and the release version in a corner. Owner idea, 2026-11-02.
- Chaos effects as Archipelago traps. CTR Native Expanded has 38 race effects, harmful and helpful (reversed steering, low gravity, giant karts, no items...), with an external trigger file. They could be the trap and buff items dowlle's tracker asks for (#248, #455, #486), sent by the multiworld instead of a timer.
- CTR Native Expanded's two-player co-op Adventure. The Archipelago client would need to know about a second player.
- More multiplayer from CTR Native Expanded: 3P/4P Arcade, eight racers in split screen, full-detail split screen, Oxide Station in multiplayer, race tracks in Battle, ending a race when one racer is left.
- Mirror mode, reverse tracks, Super/Ultra Hard, extra arcade modes, boss fights on any track (High Octane; CTR Native Expanded also has Super/Ultra Hard in Adventure, with matching relic and crystal times). Less useful for Archipelago play.
- High Octane's ghost replay tools.
- Racers from other games (low priority): Mario Kart DS karts and drivers, or other PS1 formats, as custom racers.
- Better bots (low priority): smarter driving, item use, lines and shortcuts, with no stat advantage.
- PAL disc support (low priority). Upstream removed it on purpose (ctr-native #51, #54).
- macOS (low priority). First a clean 64-bit/arm64 build (builds are 32-bit x86 MinGW32 today), then a macOS platform layer: window, GL context, audio, controllers. Until then the Windows exe runs under CrossOver/Wine with GL 3.x (ctr-native #20, #33).

## Not planned
- PS Vita and web builds, AdHoc netplay.
- High Octane's online leaderboard, which depends on its author's server.
- JP disc support. Upstream removed it on purpose (ctr-native #51).
- Upstream's MSVC build (`build-msvc.bat`, the MSVC presets in `CMakePresets.json`). The client and our platform code use GCC extensions; Windows builds use MSYS2 MinGW32.
